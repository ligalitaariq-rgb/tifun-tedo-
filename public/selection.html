Meat Quality Guide

🥩

Perfect Marbling

Look for thin white flecks of fat. This ensures your meat is tender and juicy after cooking.

🔴

Vibrant Color

Fresh meat should be bright red. Avoid meat that looks grey or has dark brown spots.

👌

Firm Texture

The meat should feel firm and spring back when touched. It should never feel slimy or soft.

👃

Fresh Aroma

Quality meat has a neutral, clean scent. Any strong or sour smell is a sign it's not fresh.
